How it works

Scan. Review. Resolve.

A full audit takes seconds. Every recommendation is reproducible, and nothing happens until you decide it should.

  1. 01

    Scan locally

    Read-only probes of memory, CPU, and storage. No account, no upload, no privileged helper.

  2. 02

    Review the evidence

    Each finding shows severity, the numbers behind it, affected paths, and what the trade-off is.

  3. 03

    Run the fix yourself

    Copy a command, open the right Settings pane, or reveal the folder. You stay in control.

Safety

Read-only is the feature.

Most Mac cleaners ask you to trust a black box with delete access. Optimizr removes that decision entirely: it observes, explains, and hands you the fix.

  • No file deletion, process killing, or settings changes
  • No network code, telemetry, account, or update pings
  • No /bin/sh, no privileged helper, no root daemon
  • Destructive commands are copy-only and clearly labeled

Questions

The honest answers.

More detail lives in the product FAQ.

Is this another Mac cleaner?

No. Optimizr never deletes, kills, or changes anything. It audits and recommends. There is no “optimize everything” button because that is how cleaners break systems.

Does it need Full Disk Access?

No. It runs as your user with normal permissions. Some protected folders may be unreadable and are skipped rather than guessed.

Why are some sizes estimates?

Directory sizes come from du and are labeled “up to”. Actual recovery depends on what you remove and whether you empty the Trash. The report never pretends to know more than it does.

Does it work on Intel Macs?

The app targets macOS 14+ and uses no Apple-silicon-only APIs. Development and testing are on Apple silicon; Intel is on the validation roadmap.

How do I install it?

Grab the latest release, or build from source: cd app && swift run Optimizr. Local bundles are ad-hoc signed, not notarized yet.

Is it open source?

Yes, MIT licensed. The scanner, the thresholds, the command runner, and this site’s source are in one repository.
